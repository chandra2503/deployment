Power Star Puneeth Rajkumar

Puneeth Rajkumar was one of the most admired actors and entertainers in Kannada cinema.

Known affectionately as Appu, he was loved by audiences for his acting, dancing, singing and humble personality.

His contribution to Kannada cinema and his connection with fans continue to inspire generations.

Lord Shiva

Lord Shiva is one of the principal deities of Hinduism and is widely revered as Mahadeva, the great god. He is associated with meditation, transformation, compassion and spiritual liberation. His iconic symbols include the trishula, damaru, crescent moon and the sacred river Ganga.
